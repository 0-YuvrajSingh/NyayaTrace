  The pandoc toolchain (archive + extracted binary) used only for DOCX conversion was removed with it.

  `scripts/build_bm25_index.py`, and the evidence for the paper's "BM25 rebuild reproduced the same top-100" claim is the
  retained comparison output (`replay/bm25_ranking_probe.json`, `bm25_deep_compare.json`, `bm25_rebuilt.json`, logs).

  retained scripts were kept even when byte-identical (e.g. `replay/e2_test_predictions.json`, read by
  `experiments/rq1/run_rq1_37.py`).
- **One-off manuscript/DOCX editing scripts** (root, `scratch/`, `experiments/audit_fixes/scripts/rebuild_final_batch*`,
  `sixpage_batch*`) were removed: they can re-insert superseded claims (5/30 as Recall@5, "held-out", 30/22/8, "81 tests").
  Their purpose is recorded here; tracked ones remain in git history.
- **Superseded manuscript-process audits** (Phase 2.x, readiness, release, scope-alignment, earlier cleanup-pass
  bookkeeping, paper drafts in `artifacts/`) were removed; the adjudications (`docs/RQ1_FINAL_ADJUDICATION.md`,
